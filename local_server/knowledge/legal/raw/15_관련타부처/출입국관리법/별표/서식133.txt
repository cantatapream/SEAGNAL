  해 주시기 바랍니다. If you are before you arrive or depart, fill the "Notice". If you definitely arrive or depart, fill the "Final". If you change   
  it, fill the "Change". If you cancel it, fill the "Cancel".

    09. 선용품 적재 ship's stores   99. 기타 etc.
